About Us

Maple Grove High School opened in 1962 and moved to its current building on Orchard Road in 1998. Students choose from 14 Advanced Placement courses, a career and technical program, and more than 30 clubs and teams.

Principal: Dana Whitfield

Maple Grove Local School District does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, sex, disability or age in its programs and activities.
